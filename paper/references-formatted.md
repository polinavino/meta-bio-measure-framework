## Notes on flagged items and editorial choices

- **Foundations of Measurement, Vol. II — year resolved to 1989.** The original Academic
  Press hardcover (and the Dover reprint's stated "1989 edition") carry 1989; the "1990"
  seen in some catalogs traces to a Psychometrika review that bundled Vols II and III
  (reviewed together, both listed San Diego: Academic Press). Vol. II author order is
  Suppes, Krantz, Luce, Tversky (differs from Vol. I), as in the trusted source.

- **Michell (1997) page range confirmed 355–383.** British Journal of Psychology 88(3),
  DOI 10.1111/j.2044-8295.1997.tb02641.x (Wiley and ProQuest both give 355 as start page;
  the occasional "335" in secondary sources is a typo).

- **iAge is listed under Sayed, N. (2021), not Furman.** The task named it "Furman et al.",
  but Furman, D. is the senior (last) author; the correct name-date lead is Sayed, N. It is
  alphabetized under S. Likewise **ipAGE** is led by Yusipov, I. (not Kalyakulina) and is
  under Y; **SImAge** is led by Kalyakulina, A. and is under K.

- A few trusted-source entries listed the venue/year but paraphrased rather than quoted the
  title, or used "et al." Titles and full author lists were completed from the primary
  records (PubMed / publisher pages): Belsky/DunedinPACE, Bland & Altman (1986, 1995), Bosc,
  Fan, Halfon & Reggiani, Kolde, Uitdehaag & Zaman. All DOIs in the list were confirmed
  against a primary or publisher record this session.
