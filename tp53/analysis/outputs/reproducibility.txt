  Giacomelli signs fixed in advance: [-1, -1, -1] (fitness -> deleteriousness)

(1) Spearman(consensus_yeast, consensus_mammalian):
    overall +0.487 | extremes +0.627 | middle -0.054
    per-tertile (same range restriction in each, so comparable):
      bottom (functional)    +0.008  (n=770)
      middle                 -0.054  (n=768)
      top (deleterious)      +0.561  (n=770)
    pooled extremes - middle: +0.681  bootstrap 95% CI [+0.605, +0.760]
    mean(functional,deleterious) - middle: +0.338  bootstrap 95% CI [+0.254, +0.423]
    (The agreement at the extremes is carried by the deleterious end; the functional
     end agrees no better than the middle, so this is a floor effect shared by both
     platforms, not a symmetric extremes-versus-middle pattern.)
